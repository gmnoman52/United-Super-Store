# United Super Store colour palette

Direction: friendly, dependable, familiar. Android first, delivery and pickup.
The approved green logo anchors the palette. Its raster colour may vary slightly; #075525 is the interface brand token.

Use neutral backgrounds and white surfaces. Reserve green for primary actions, selected navigation, focus, and the logo. Use selectedSurface behind selected controls with brand-coloured text. Use three text roles. Divider is decorative only; controlBorder identifies input boundaries. Semantic colours require text or icons as well as colour. No gradients, glows, coloured shadows, dummy products, invented prices or offers.

Tokens: colors.json is the shared source for the future native theme.
Primary buttons: onBrand over brand; pressed state uses brandPressed.
Body copy: textPrimary, textSecondary or textMuted on background or surface.
Success, warning and error text appear on surface; status names accompany them.

Guidance applied: UI/UX Pro Max accessibility and token consistency; Hallmark token discipline; Unslop brand preservation, quiet neutrals and restrained accent.
